📦 TEXTURE FILES LOCATION
========================

Please place your three kitchen texture images in this directory:

Required files:
- kitchen1.png
- kitchen2.png
- kitchen3.png

These images should contain:
✓ Kitchen booth/background
✓ Cutting board
✓ Knife
✓ Wok
✓ Vegetables basket
✓ Bottles (soy sauce, oil)
✓ Bowl with ingredients
✓ Trash bin
✓ Green bowl
✓ Stove + wok with ingredients

The game will automatically load these textures when you open index.html!
